**Status:** selected for buyer discovery; not yet a validated purchase.
**Core opportunity:** evidence integrity inside a real, already-monetized logistics finance workflow.
**Vertical:** freight invoice audit / overcharge evidence, not AI tooling.
**SABLE role:** evidence closure and provenance for an auditor's already-identified exception. Do not compete on rate calculation, claim filing or recovery.

## What not to build

Do NOT create another generic freight audit engine, invoice OCR product, or AI claim filing agent. Existing suppliers already advertise those functions. Do not claim 3–7% recovery based on market pages or imply that every flagged line is collectible.

## Differentiated hypothesis

Freight audit specialists already identify candidate discrepancies. A secondary **Exception Evidence Closure Pack** checks whether each already-flagged exception can be reconstructed and reviewed without searching across disconnected files:
- one exception → one claim being evaluated → authorized evidence list;
- each amount/identifier/date bound to a source document, page/row, and source hash;
- mathematical recalculation recorded separately from contractual interpretation;
- a four-state evidence verdict: SUPPORTED / CONTRADICTED / INCOMPLETE / CONFLICT;
- missing-source list and concise reviewer handoff;
- reproducible manifest and clear limitations.

This is a reviewer-side evidence QA layer, not a determination that a carrier owes a refund and not legal advice.

## Target customer and buying logic

**First buyer:** founder/operations lead of a boutique freight-audit/recovery provider handling recurring LTL, accessorial, or ocean invoice disputes, with a human reviewer who still manually assembles evidence before a case can be submitted.

**Economic reason to buy:** reduce reviewer time per exception, increase proportion of findings that have complete evidence, and avoid escalating weakly supported claims. These benefits are hypotheses until measured in a real pilot.

## Paid-pilot offer to test

**US$1,500 fixed-scope evidence-closure pilot (proposed, unvalidated):**
- 10 already-flagged exceptions;
- buyer supplies anonymized/redacted invoice lines, contract/rate excerpts and shipment references;
- up to 30 source files;
- source-linked evidence matrix, recalculation trace, missing/conflicting item list and reviewer-ready pack;
- no filing, no carrier contact, no legal or customs advice, no access to production systems, no funds handled;
- 5 business-day delivery after agreed complete input;
- acceptance based on traceability, reproducibility, and reviewer time measured by the buyer—not recovered-money promises.

Offer monthly service only after one pilot proves measurable savings and the buyer requests recurring use. Test $1,500/month for a bounded monthly batch only after pilot, not before. Avoid contingent recovery fees or taking a share of claims.

## Safety and commercial rules

- Start with synthetic data; live pilot requires written NDA/data terms, minimal redacted data, secure transfer and deletion schedule. If safe data handling cannot be confirmed, do not accept files.
- Do not authorize short-pay, dispute filings, collection actions, or carrier communications. Customer's own authorized reviewer makes decisions and submits claims.
- No guarantee that a charge is invalid or recoverable. Separate observable data integrity from contractual/legal conclusions.
- Do not promise a legal deadline based only on an invoice date; deadlines depend on contract, mode, jurisdiction, and applicable rules and require the client's specialist.
- No integrations, broad platform, or further feature work until one buyer confirms a current bottleneck, budget, payer and lawful payment route.

## Commercial gate
Proceed only after a target confirms:
1. Current recurring workload, not hypothetical interest.
2. Manually assembled evidence creates a measurable delay, rework or review cost.
3. A named owner has authority to purchase a $1,500 bounded pilot.
4. An anonymized/redacted sample can lawfully be shared.
5. The proposed output is not already fully handled by their current tools/process.
6. Payment path to the seller is legal, available and confirmed.

No budget/payer confirmation = not a qualified deal. No settled funds = R3 remains zero.
