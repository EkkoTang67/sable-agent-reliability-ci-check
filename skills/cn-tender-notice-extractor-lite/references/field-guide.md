# Field guide: source and deadline extraction

## Source quality
- official_original: official buyer, procurement agent or official procurement platform page is accessible and the relevant current notice is visible.
- aggregator: repost, search result, content farm or procurement-information mirror.
- user_provided: only a user-supplied excerpt/image/file was reviewed.
- unknown: provenance cannot be determined.

## Current status
- open_verified: official source confirms the relevant action remains open for the specified participant/context.
- closed_verified: relevant deadline passed, notice was cancelled/closed, or the required access window closed for a participant who has not already obtained the documents.
- changed_or_cancelled: an official amendment, correction, cancellation, or replacement changes the original notice.
- unknown: original source or current status not checked.

## Date checklist
Always distinguish document access from bid submission. A bid deadline in the future does not prove a new participant can still enter if document access/registration has already closed. Conversely, a closed document-access window may not prevent a previously registered bidder from submitting. State the assumed participant situation.

## Evidence citations
Prefer short quotations and section/page locators over untraceable summaries. Keep each claim linked to its source. Never infer that a missing clause means zero cost, no bond, unrestricted subcontracting, or guaranteed payment.
