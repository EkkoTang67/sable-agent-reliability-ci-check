## Current primary bet
Construction payment-application readiness remains the first active test because:
- the buyer/channel sells recurring accounting operations;
- the workflow is repeated across a client portfolio;
- a source-linked readiness report can be delivered without production system integration;
- test data can be redacted and the tool does not need access to funds;
- the output can be kept strictly to evidence completeness and arithmetic without making legal/accounting determinations.

This is a hypothesis, not a conclusion that this is the best market. First actual payer evidence may reverse the ranking.

## Offer under test
**Payment Application Readiness Pack — US$1,500 pilot**
- Three client-provided/redacted payment application or closeout packages.
- Up to 30 files.
- Source-linked matrix, arithmetic/cross-document discrepancy notes, timeline and missing/unclear evidence.
- Delivered within 5 business days after the inputs and scope are complete.
- Acceptance based on reproducibility, source traceability, gap identification, and measured prep/rework time.
- Does not certify completion, determine legal entitlement, validate lien waiver law, advise on lien rights, contact GC/owner, submit the application or guarantee payment.

After one pilot, only if measurable utility is proven and the channel asks to repeat, test **US$2,500/month** for a specified bounded monthly batch. The recurring price is a hypothesis, not market-validated.

## Immediate sequence
1. Wait for and classify any replies from the three targeted contacts; do not send immediate duplicate messages.
2. For any response, ask only the deal-critical questions: live case, current money/decision consequence, blocker, deadline, owner, budget authority, lawful redacted data path, acceptance metric, legal payment path.
3. If no replies, do not wait indefinitely. Find a second set of 5 tightly matched accounts via official service pages and role-specific contacts; personalize to one concrete public workflow each.
4. If a buyer confirms a real case, agree scope, NDA/data handling, deletion window, payment method and acceptance criteria before receiving sensitive records or beginning work.
5. Collect a fixed pilot fee in advance only if payment is legal/operationally available; verify actual settlement into an account controlled by the seller.
6. Deliver only synthetic samples until redacted customer data can be handled lawfully and securely.
7. Offer recurring service only after one paid pilot shows measurable value and the buyer requests repeat usage.

## Commercial risk controls
- No debt, paid ads, upfront infrastructure spend, inventory, fund custody, production write access, or recovery contingency claims.
- Keep initial data synthetic; only accept redacted customer data with written scope, permission, secure transfer and deletion schedule.
- No legal, tax, accounting sign-off or professional certification. SABLE reports what the submitted evidence does/doesn't establish.
- No guaranteed recovery, no legal deadline claims, no direct carrier/GC/retailer submissions.
- Do not misstate citizenship, location, entity, or payment eligibility. Verify the buyer's and seller's actual permitted payment rails before an engagement.
- Don't confuse positive replies, budgets, invoices, or payment initiation with settled revenue.

## Kill rules
Kill an opportunity if: no live case; it can be resolved internally with no material cost; incumbent tools already close the gap; no payer/budget/deadline; unsafe or unlawful data handling; cross-border settlement unavailable; or the buyer wants the product to perform unauthorized production or legal actions.
