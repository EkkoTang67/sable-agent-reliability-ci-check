# SABLE opportunity: construction funding evidence closure

**Status:** prospecting hypothesis, not a lending or underwriting service.
**Target prospect:** specialty finance firm that advances funds against approved pay applications, retainage, or materials.
**Public prospect example:** Cartwheel Funding describes a flow of submit documents → verify project/GC/contract terms → fund → collect when owner/GC pays; its public page lists Alex@cartwheelfunding.com as contact. This is public-fit evidence only, not confirmed willingness to buy.
Source: https://www.cartwheelfunding.com/

## The specific problem to test
Before a finance provider advances money on a contractor's submitted pay-app or retainage package, someone needs to reconcile the key facts in the submitted documents:
- Is the stated project/contract/party identifier consistent across the supplied documents?
- Does the approved pay-app amount match the summary/detail schedule?
- Are approval, submission, and release states supported by dated source records?
- Are key required documents present, current, and tied to the same project/payment period?
- Where do two source artifacts disagree, and what exact source is missing?

A green dashboard or a borrower statement is not itself proof of the underlying state. However, SABLE does not decide creditworthiness, entitlement, lien validity, legal sufficiency, or whether a financing request should be approved.

## Proposed deliverable
**Pre-Funding Evidence Closure Pack** — one scoped, source-linked evidence map for a financing case the provider already intends to review:
- evidence inventory, source/page/row, date, issuer, and a hash where feasible;
- project / contract / pay-app identity consistency;
- arithmetic checks and amount reconciliation, with formula/source references;
- PRESENT / MISSING / CONFLICTING / UNCLEAR evidence status;
- chronology from supplied records;
- explicit unresolved questions for the funding firm's own authorized underwriter;
- provenance manifest and limitations.

## Commercial hypothesis
Proposed first pilot: **US$2,500 for five redacted funding cases**, with a 5-business-day delivery target after the complete, authorized set is received. Acceptance must be based on reproducibility and time spent by the provider's own reviewer, not a promise to prevent losses or increase approvals.

Do not offer ongoing monthly service until a paid pilot proves measurable utility. If the buyer confirms repeated cases, test a bounded monthly evidence-review allowance at a negotiated recurring price. No credit-decision automation, no underwriting score, no fund handling, no guarantee.

## Why this may support greater value than generic QA
The evidence is upstream of a funding decision that can release capital. The potential buyer operates a repeatable finance workflow rather than buying a one-time report. These are economic-fit hypotheses; public marketing copy is not evidence that they have a manual bottleneck or budget for this tool.

## Data, legal, and risk constraints
- Synthetic example only until secure data handling is contractually approved.
- Written permitted purpose, scope, NDA / data-processing terms where appropriate, redaction rules, secure transfer and deletion window required for live cases.
- No bank credentials, full SSNs/tax IDs, consumer credit data, personal data irrelevant to the stated checks, or production-system access.
- No credit advice, underwriting conclusion, appraisal, loan brokering, legal opinion, lien opinion, claim submission, funds custody, disbursement, collection, or direct counterparty contact.
- The finance provider retains exclusive authority for its financing decision. SABLE reports evidence and conflicts only.
- Verify the buyer/seller can lawfully contract and use a working cross-border payment path before accepting the case.
- Any missing safe data route or lawful payment route = no pilot.

## Qualification gate
Proceed only after the prospect confirms:
1. They fund actual pay-app/retainage/material cases in recurring volume.
2. A specific evidence reconciliation gap exists before funding.
3. That gap costs reviewer time or affects a current funding decision.
4. They have a named budget owner and authority for a US$2,500 test.
5. They can provide 5 lawful redacted examples.
6. They can name measurable acceptance criteria.
7. They can pay through an available, lawful route.

Nothing beyond current public workflow fit is confirmed. No customer, budget, or revenue is claimed. R3 unchanged until funds settle into seller-controlled account.
