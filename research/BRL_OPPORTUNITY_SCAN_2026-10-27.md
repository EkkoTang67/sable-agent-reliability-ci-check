## Candidate A — n8n paid QA / stress-test request

**Primary source:** https://community.n8n.io/t/looking-for-n8n-ai-automation-specialist-paid-qa-stress-testing/319256

**FACT (2026-10-06):** The topic author wrote that they had already built several AI agents using n8n, Vapi, Twilio, ElevenLabs, OpenAI/Claude and APIs/webhooks. The author asked for review/stress testing before demos to paying clients, including bugs, failure points, edge cases, error handling, integration and AI behavior. The author said the agents should not be rebuilt from scratch, offered to start with one agent as a paid trial, and mentioned possible ongoing work. The author invited people to DM with time zone, rate, relevant experience, voice-platform experience and examples.

**FACT (observed thread):** The topic accumulated hundreds of public replies, with later thread pages showing activity through approximately 2026-10-21. Other applicants already proposed concrete test matrices, demonstrations, rates and reports.

**UNKNOWN / NOT_VERIFIED as of 2026-10-27:**
- Whether the author is still looking or has already selected someone.
- Whether the paid trial remains funded and open.
- Budget/range, start date, delivery deadline and acceptance criteria.
- Whether the author will consider a workflow/API-only test rather than a full production voice-agent test.
- Payment method and whether an individual contractor in mainland China can be onboarded and paid.
- Whether the account can post a reply or DM through the forum's current permission settings.
- No buyer response or selection was established from the reviewed public thread.

**Inference:** The initial post is substantially stronger than a generic “AI companies may need QA” signal because it describes a particular work package and explicitly states an intention to pay for one trial. It is nevertheless a crowded, potentially stale lead. A generic application is unlikely to differentiate.

**Game-theory view:** The buyer has a broad set of outside options; applicants are offering detailed samples and low prices. We have no demonstrated Vapi/Twilio/ElevenLabs production credentials to claim, so competing on broad expertise or lowest price would be weak and potentially misleading. A bounded workflow/API failure-reproduction job could fit only if the buyer accepts that scope. Do not provide free bespoke tests before scope and paid terms are agreed.

**Gate:** Not PAYABLE-NOW. The only justified next action is a minimal status/qualification check via the same forum topic because the author explicitly invited responses there, provided platform rules and account access permit it. Ask whether the trial is still open and, if so, request the budget range, deadline, acceptance criteria, required voice-platform depth, and permitted payment/onboarding route. Do not spend time building another demo unless the author confirms the trial is open and scope fits.

**Suggested message (NOT SENT):**

> Is the one-agent paid trial still open, or have you already selected someone? Before I propose a scope, could you confirm the budget range, target demo/deadline, acceptance criteria, and whether a workflow/API reliability pass is useful independently of live voice-quality testing? I would only quote after confirming the sanitized workflow scope and a viable contractor/payment route; I would not claim production Vapi/ElevenLabs credentials I cannot substantiate.

If no reply or the position is closed, park/kill promptly. Do not duplicate public claims or portfolio identities belonging to other applicants.

## Hard rule

A public request, published price, partner page, contract award, or number of outreach attempts does not equal an actual purchase. The target remains **a bounded work package, a buyer with spending authority, mutually accepted acceptance evidence, a viable legal/payment path, and finally cleared funds**.
