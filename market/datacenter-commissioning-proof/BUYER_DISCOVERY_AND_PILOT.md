## Qualification before work
Proceed only if all are established:
- a live, current closeout package and decision deadline;
- a real evidence reconciliation cost/bottleneck;
- named budget owner and written approval;
- documented acceptance criteria;
- lawful sharing of redacted records, NDA/data handling and deletion;
- no request for live OT access, equipment controls, safety sign-off, or engineering judgment outside scope;
- legally available payment route for the seller and funds actually settle before any paid work starts.

## Fail fast
If all gaps are already handled by their current Cx platform or manual workflow, kill the offer and ask which adjacent closure remains unsolved. Do not build integrations until the buyer provides evidence of the problem and the path to budget.
