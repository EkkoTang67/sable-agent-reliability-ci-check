# SABLE vertical offer: construction payment-application readiness

**Status:** primary buyer-discovery experiment. No buyer, contract, or revenue has been confirmed.
**Industry:** US commercial construction finance (non-AI end market).
**Buyer:** boutique construction accounting / fractional-CFO firms that manage AIA billing, retainage, WIP and lien-waiver records for many contractors.
**Beneficiary:** contractors with rejected or delayed payment applications, or retainage/final-payment decisions blocked by missing or inconsistent documents.

## Why this market is real
- FinRise Advisors' September 14, 2026 article says a $4M contractor may have $200k–$400k retained (a 5–10% illustrative range) and describes retainage being miscoded or not tied cleanly to jobs. This is an illustrative claim from a service provider, not a universal average. https://www.finriseadvisors.com/blog/retainage-accounting-for-contractors
- FinRise's AIA billing guide states G702 cover-sheet values must tie to G703 detail, and a math error can cause the whole application to be returned for resubmission. https://www.finriseadvisors.com/blog/aia-billing-explained
- FinRise's services include construction accounting, AIA G702/G703 billing, retainage and lien-waiver management for contractor clients in the $2M–$50M+ revenue bands. https://www.finriseadvisors.com/services
- Builder CFO publishes monthly construction-finance packages at $500, $1,200, and $2,500 per month. This is a clear signal that the buyer segment sells recurring financial operations; it is not evidence that they will buy SABLE. https://www.buildercfo.co/services

## Product: Payment Application Readiness Pack
Not an AI product sold to AI buyers. SABLE is the evidence engine behind a specific finance workflow.

For one payment-application package, compare the client-supplied requirement checklist to redacted source records and return:
- a line-by-line readiness status: PRESENT / MISSING / CONFLICTING / UNCLEAR;
- arithmetic and cross-document consistency checks, separately labeled from interpretations;
- source references for the amounts and status fields checked;
- a chronology of submitted/approved/revised artifacts;
- a concise list of blocking questions for the authorized construction-finance reviewer;
- a reproducible evidence manifest and explicit limitations.

The pack does not decide whether a lien waiver is legally valid, interpret state-specific lien rights, certify work completion, decide what is contractually owed, submit a payment application, sign a waiver, or guarantee that funds will be released.

## Commercial model to test
**Pilot (proposed): US$1,500 fixed scope for three redacted payment-application/closeout packages**, using a buyer-supplied checklist and no more than 30 files total. Target turnaround: 5 business days after complete, agreed inputs. Acceptance criteria: the buyer can reproduce each marked discrepancy; every finding has source support; missing evidence is identified; and a timed reviewer comparison shows whether the pack reduces prep/rework time.

**Recurring channel model (hypothesis, not yet offered as a proven subscription):** US$2,500/month for a bounded batch of up to 20 packages for one construction accounting/fractional-CFO firm, only after a paid pilot demonstrates measurable savings and the firm asks for recurring capacity. This is a pricing experiment, not validated willingness-to-pay.

A channel firm could include the readiness review in its existing monthly service or charge it as a premium add-on. Do not assert that any prospect has agreed to resell it.

## Why this may support higher-value cash flow
The proposed buyer already sells recurring accounting/CFO services and may manage multiple contractor clients. If the pack measurably lowers manual review time or rejected resubmissions, one channel customer could supply repeated monthly work across a client portfolio. That is the operating hypothesis; only a signed paid pilot and repeated use can validate it.

## Data, safety and legal boundaries
- Start with the synthetic sample only. Live pilot requires written scope, permitted data use, secure transfer, a retention/deletion window, and preferably client-approved redaction.
- Do not accept tax IDs, bank credentials, payroll/worker personal data, private customer data, contract material beyond what is strictly needed, or access to a live accounting system.
- Do not decide legal sufficiency of a waiver or release; check only the buyer's stated document type/field/status and flag unknowns for their qualified reviewer.
- No legal advice, lien filing, collection activity, direct contact with GCs/owners, accounting sign-off, payment initiation or handling client funds.
- If lawful cross-border data handling or payment from buyer to seller cannot be confirmed, don't accept the case.

## Proceed / stop gate
Proceed only if a named partner confirms a current recurring bottleneck, the owner of the budget, lawful sharing of a redacted test set, measurable acceptance criteria, and a valid payment path. If not, kill this wedge and move to another sector. No reply or positive conversation counts as revenue; R3 requires funds actually settled into a seller-controlled account.
