## First message — short and specific
Subject: A 10-case evidence-closure pilot for your freight audit team

Body:
Hello,

I saw that your team already audits freight invoices and prepares evidence-backed findings. I'm testing a narrow add-on rather than another audit engine: for exceptions your reviewer has already flagged, SABLE packages the exact source references, recalculation trace, and missing/conflicting evidence so a reviewer can reproduce the finding without reassembling the file.

The proposed pilot is US$1,500 fixed scope for 10 already-flagged exceptions, using redacted records only. It excludes claim filing, carrier contact, legal/customs advice, system access and any promise of recovery. Acceptance is based on source traceability, reproducibility and reviewer time saved.

Is evidence assembly still a manual bottleneck for your reviewers, and is there a named owner with budget for a bounded pilot? If the current workflow already solves this, I'll close the loop and won't keep pitching.

Regards,
SABLE — Independent Verification

## Qualifying questions (must be explicit)
- How many flagged exceptions does one reviewer assemble evidence for each month?
- How many minutes per exception, including missing-document follow-up and rework?
- What percentage is returned or rejected for insufficient supporting evidence?
- Which step is not already handled by the present platform?
- Who owns the budget and can authorize a US$1,500 test?
- Can a small redacted dataset be shared lawfully and securely?
- What measurable result makes the paid pilot a success?
- Is there a legal, operational and cross-border payment route available to pay the seller?

## Stop rule
If they confirm no manual evidence bottleneck, no budget, no payer, no lawful data-sharing path, or no accessible payment path, stop. Do not start a software integration project to manufacture demand.
